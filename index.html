CAMPUS COMMUNITY

Student & teacher support

Separate issue types for students, teaching staff and campus employees.

♙

Student concerns

Report issues affecting learning, access to facilities or everyday campus life.

  • Classroom and timetable issues
  • Library and study-space access
  • Hostel, transport and canteen concerns
  • Accessibility and campus safety
  • IT, Wi-Fi and student service issues
▣

Teacher concerns

Report issues affecting teaching, practicals and academic operations.

  • Teaching equipment and projectors
  • Lab resources and maintenance
  • Classroom scheduling conflicts
  • Faculty workspace and IT issues
  • Academic facility requests
⚙

Staff & service concerns

Raise maintenance and operational issues that need campus coordination.

  • Cleaning and waste collection
  • Electrical and plumbing repairs
  • Security and access equipment
  • Furniture and building repairs
  • Grounds and transport facilities
Privacy note

This prototype is not a secure confidential reporting channel. Do not submit allegations, medical details or sensitive personal information. A real deployment needs access controls and a protected backend.
